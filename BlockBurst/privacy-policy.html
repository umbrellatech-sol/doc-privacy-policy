Block Burst

Privacy Policy — Effective Date: September 20, 2026

What Block Burst Does

Block Burst is a free block-puzzle game. You drag block shapes onto an 8×8 grid and full rows and columns burst. It has a Classic endless mode, an Adventure mode with levels and stars, and a coin economy with boosters. The game plays fully offline.

Data We Collect

Anonymous usage and crash data

Block Burst uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (by Google). Together they collect:

  • Which screens you open and how long you stay on them
  • In-game events such as starting and finishing a level, using a booster, or buying one with coins
  • Your device model, operating-system version, country and language
  • An app-instance identifier that identifies the installation, not you
  • If the app crashes: the crash report and the state of the app at that moment

We use this only to see which parts of the game people play and to fix crashes. It is not sold, and it is not used to advertise to you.

What We Do Not Collect

No name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any account information. Block Burst has no sign-in and no user accounts.

What Stays On Your Device

Your high score, level progress and stars, daily-challenge results and streak, coin balance, booster inventory, play-time medals, and settings are stored only in the app's local storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

There are no ads and no in-app purchases in this version. No data is shared with advertisers.

Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy
