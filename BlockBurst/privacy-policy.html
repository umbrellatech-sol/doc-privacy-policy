Block Burst

Privacy Policy — Effective Date: October 13, 2026

What Block Burst Does

Block Burst is a free-to-play block-puzzle game with optional in-app purchases. You drag block shapes onto an 8×8 grid and full rows and columns burst. It has a Classic endless mode, an Adventure mode with levels and stars, and a coin economy with boosters. The game plays fully offline.

Data We Collect

Anonymous usage and crash data

Block Burst uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (by Google). Together they collect:

  • Which screens you open and how long you stay on them
  • In-game events such as starting and finishing a level, using a booster, or buying one with coins
  • Your device model, operating-system version, country and language
  • An app-instance identifier that identifies the installation, not you
  • If the app crashes: the crash report and the state of the app at that moment

We use this only to see which parts of the game people play and to fix crashes. It is not sold, and it is not used to advertise to you.

Block Burst also uses Firebase Remote Config (by Google) to receive game settings, such as the dates and rewards of in-game events. To do this it sends the app-instance identifier, app version, device type, country and language to Google, so the right settings can be delivered. Nothing about how you play is sent this way.

What We Do Not Collect

No name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any account information. Block Burst has no sign-in and no user accounts.

What Stays On Your Device

Your high score, level progress and stars, daily-challenge results and streak, coin balance, booster inventory, play-time medals, the items you have bought, and settings are stored only in the app's local storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Internet Required by Analytics and Crashlytics to send the data described above, by Remote Config to receive game settings, and by Google Play to complete purchases. The game itself plays fully offline.

In-App Purchases

Block Burst offers optional purchases of coins and booster bundles. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your name, card, or other payment details. Google Play tells the app only which product was bought, so it can be delivered. Purchases are covered by Google Play's terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

There are no ads in this version. No data is shared with advertisers.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Remote Config — game settings such as in-game events (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases (Google Privacy Policy)

Google is the processor of this data, under the Firebase data-processing terms.

Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Google Play, where family and parental purchase controls apply.

Changes to This Policy

If what the app collects ever changes, this policy is updated before the change ships, with a new effective date, and the store listing declarations are updated with it.
