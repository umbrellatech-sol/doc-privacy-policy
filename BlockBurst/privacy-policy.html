Block Burst

Privacy Policy — Effective Date: October 17, 2026

What Block Burst Does

Block Burst is a free-to-play block-puzzle game with optional in-app purchases. The game may show ads. You drag block shapes onto an 8×8 grid and full rows and columns burst. It has a Classic endless mode, an Adventure mode with levels and stars, and a coin economy with boosters. The game plays fully offline.

Data We Collect

Usage, crash and advertising data

Block Burst uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (by Google). Together they collect:

  • Which screens you open and how long you stay on them
  • In-game events such as starting and finishing a level, using a booster, or buying one with coins
  • Your device model, operating-system version, country and language
  • An app-instance identifier that identifies the installation, not you
  • If the app crashes: the crash report and the state of the app at that moment

We use this to see which parts of the game people play, to fix crashes, and to measure how the ads in the game perform. It is not sold.

Block Burst also uses Firebase Remote Config (by Google) to receive game settings, such as the dates and rewards of in-game events. To do this it sends the app-instance identifier, app version, device type, country and language to Google, so the right settings can be delivered. Nothing about how you play is sent this way.

If the game shows ads, Google AdMob collects the data described under Advertising below.

What We Do Not Collect

No name, email address, contacts, photos, location from GPS, or any account information. Block Burst has no sign-in and no user accounts.

What Stays On Your Device

Your high score, level progress and stars, daily-challenge results and streak, coin balance, booster inventory, play-time medals, the items you have bought, and settings are stored only in the app's local storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

In-App Purchases

Block Burst offers optional purchases of coins and booster bundles, and a purchase that removes ads. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your name, card, or other payment details. Google Play tells the app only which product was bought, so it can be delivered. Purchases are covered by Google Play's terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Block Burst may show ads from Google AdMob and its partner ad networks:

  • Rewarded ads — only when you choose to watch one, for example to revive a game or double your coins. The reward is shown before the ad starts.
  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads between games or levels, never during play.
  • Banner ads — on some menu screens, never on the play screen.

The Remove Ads purchase removes interstitial and banner ads. Rewarded ads stay available because you choose to watch them.

What AdMob collects

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Device information such as model, operating-system version, language and screen size
  • Your approximate location, based on your IP address
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps

Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud. With your consent, ads can be personalised to your interests. Without consent, you see non-personalised ads, which are based only on the context, such as the app and your general location. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent and choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message (IAB TCF v2.2). You can accept, choose which purposes and partners to allow, or decline personalised ads.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and Utah), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Change your choice at any time: open Settings → Privacy options in the game. This option appears when it applies in your region.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to device Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset or delete it.

For data that Google holds, you can make access or deletion requests through your Google account or the Google Privacy Policy. We do not hold any personal data ourselves.
