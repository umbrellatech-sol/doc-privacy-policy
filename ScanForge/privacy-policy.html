ScanForge

Privacy Policy — Effective Date: May 9, 2026

What ScanForge Does

ScanForge is a QR code and barcode scanner and generator app. It lets you:

  • Scan barcodes and QR codes using your camera or from gallery images
  • Generate barcodes and QR codes in 10 formats
  • Save scan history on your device
  • Mark scans as favorites

Data We Collect

Minimal & Anonymous Only

ScanForge uses Firebase Analytics (by Google) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics — such as which screens are visited and which features are used. This data contains no personally identifiable information, no barcode content, and no camera images. It is used solely to improve the app.

ScanForge uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports when the app crashes. These reports help us fix bugs and contain no personal data.

We do not collect your name, email, location, camera images, scanned content, or any other personal information. We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising purposes.

How the App Uses Your Device

PermissionWhy
Camera To scan barcodes and QR codes. Camera images are processed on-device only and never saved or transmitted.
Vibration To give haptic feedback when a scan is successful.
Storage (read) To let you pick an image from your gallery and scan a barcode from it.
Storage (write) To save generated barcode images to your device gallery when you choose to.

Local Storage

All scan history, favorites, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android's built-in database (Room) and preferences (DataStore). This data never leaves your device.

Internet Access

ScanForge requires no internet connection for scanning or generation — it works fully offline. The only network traffic is the anonymous analytics and crash reports described above, sent by Firebase over HTTPS.

Third-Party Services

ScanForge uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Crashlytics — anonymous crash reporting (Google Privacy Policy)

There are no ads, no ad SDKs, and no user tracking of any kind.

Open Source Libraries

  • ZXing — barcode scanning and generation (Apache 2.0)
  • CameraX — camera preview (Apache 2.0)
  • Room — local database (Apache 2.0)
  • DataStore — local preferences (Apache 2.0)
  • Coil — image loading (Apache 2.0)
  • Jetpack Compose — UI framework (Apache 2.0)
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — analytics and crash reporting (Google Terms)

Children's Privacy

ScanForge does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only data collected is anonymous and aggregated (Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics), which contains no personal information.
